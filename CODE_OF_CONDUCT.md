# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make taking part in this project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- accepting constructive criticism gracefully;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- trolling, insults, derogatory comments and personal attacks;
- publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other channel associated with the project.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project maintainer at **alasdair@negroniventurestudios.com**. Every complaint will be reviewed and investigated, and will receive a response appropriate to the circumstances.
